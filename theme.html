Autonomous Vehicle Systems

The Vision: Autonomous Vehicle Systems

Our project operates under the broad theme of Autonomous Vehicle Systems — designing and developing intelligent, self-navigating ground vehicles capable of perceiving their environment, making real-time decisions, and executing precise motion control without human intervention.

Project Goal Overview: To develop a fully autonomous Unmanned Ground Vehicle (UGV) that integrates sensor fusion, path planning, and adaptive control for reliable navigation in outdoor and semi-structured environments.

Key Focus Areas

🚗 Autonomous Navigation GPS-waypoint following and real-time path planning without human input.
📡 Sensor Fusion Integrating IMU, GPS, LiDAR and camera data for robust environment perception.
🧠 Intelligent Control MATLAB Simulink-based control architectures for dynamic decision making.
🔁 Real-Time Systems Low-latency control loops for stable, responsive vehicle operation.
